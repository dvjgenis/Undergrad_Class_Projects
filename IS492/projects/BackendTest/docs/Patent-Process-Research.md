## 1. Software Patent Process (USA)

### Costs (2024–2026)

| Stage | Cost Range | Notes |
|-------|------------|-------|
| **Provisional patent** | $65–$500 | DIY to attorney-assisted; secures priority date |
| **Utility patent (USPTO fees)** | $400–$2,000 | Varies by entity size (micro/small/large) |
| **Attorney drafting** | $5,000–$15,000+ | For non-provisional utility patent |
| **Total lifecycle** | $8,000–$30,000 | Filing through grant |
| **Micro-entity** | ~80% discount | On USPTO fees; best for qualifying startups |

**2025 fee changes:** USPTO raised fees ~7–10% (effective Jan 19, 2025).

### Timeline

- **2–3 years** typical from filing to grant
- Depends on office actions, rejections, and prosecution complexity

### Hurdles

- **Alice/Mayo (35 U.S.C. § 101):** Software patents face high rejection rates. Courts rejected ~85% of software patent appeals in recent years. Claims must show **specific technological improvement** beyond generic computer use; avoid "applying known techniques to a new scenario."
- **Recent rulings (2024–2025):** Federal Circuit and PTAB have rejected AI/ML patents that merely apply standard ML methods without meaningful technical advance. HarmonyForge's **axiomatic, rule-based CSP approach** (not ML) may fare better—emphasize deterministic innovation, structural integrity, and pedagogical scaffolding.
- **Prior art:** Conduct a prior art search before filing. HarmonySolver, SchenkComposer, ComposerX, and similar systems are relevant prior art.
- **Strategy:** File a **provisional patent** first ($65–$500) to lock in a priority date, then decide on a full utility patent as the product stabilizes.

## 2. UIUC / University of Illinois IP Policy

### Ownership

Under UIUC General Rules and Article XII of the University Statutes:

1. **University employees:** IP developed in the performance of their work → University owns.
2. **Anyone (including students):** IP created using **University resources** → University owns.

**University resources** include: staff, students, facilities, equipment, and funds.

### Students and Class Projects

- **Research lab work:** Use of lab facilities/resources → typically **University ownership**.
- **Class/course projects:** Students **can** own IP if a **written exception** has been granted in advance by the Vice Chancellor for Research (e.g., industrial design, senior design).
- **Key:** Prior written approval is required for student ownership; otherwise, OTM (Office of Technology Management) makes the determination.

### HarmonyForge Context

- **INFO 490** (seminar) + **Salt Lab** (research group) = hybrid class + lab project.
- Uses faculty oversight (Dr. Huang), lab/doctoral support, and possibly university resources (LLM credits, facilities).
- **Action required:** Submit an **invention disclosure** to UIUC OTM. OTM will decide ownership and whether the University has an interest.

### If University Owns

- **IP-Linc program:** Startup-friendly licensing for University-owned IP; streamlined terms for Illini-run companies.
- **Revenue sharing:** Universities generally share licensing revenue with inventors; UIUC has policies governing inventor shares.
- **Do you have to share?** If University resources were used, the University typically has an ownership interest. You do not "share" a patent with the University—the University may **own** it, and you may receive a share of revenue as inventor.

### If Students Own (Exception Granted)

- Possible if the project qualifies for a course exception and approval was obtained in advance.
- Requires formal exception from the Vice Chancellor for Research.

## 4. Recommended Next Steps

1. **Contact UIUC OTM** (otm.illinois.edu): Disclose HarmonyForge, request ownership determination.
2. **Consider provisional filing:** If you intend to patent and have student/ownership clarity, a low-cost provisional secures priority.
3. **Prior art search:** Document HarmonySolver, SchenkComposer, ComposerX, MusicAIR, and related systems before drafting claims.
4. **Draft claims around technical advance:** Emphasize the **axiomatic hierarchy**, **deterministic CSP solver**, **Theory Inspector integration**, and **edit-authority workflow**—not generic "AI for music."
