# HarmonyForge Product Spec

**Target Personas**

---

## 1. Gigging Musician

**Needs:** Fast, reliable arrangements for Random Ensembles; time-crunch.

**Scenario:** Violinist must cover cello part for tonight's gig.

**Key features:** Multi-instrument selection, chord charts & tablature, accurate XML/PDF export, audio playback verification.

---

## 2. Music Educator / Student

**Needs:** Theory Inspector as scaffolding partner; learn why, not just what.

**Scenario:** Student asks "Why is this note marked red?"

**Key features:** Theory Inspector chatbot, Red Line validation, ante-hoc explanations, pedagogical reasoning steps.

---

## 3. Chamber Ensemble Coordinator

**Needs:** Repertoire expansion; reduce Mechanical Toil; enable collective jamming.

**Scenario:** Add ad-hoc instruments to existing arrangements.

**Key features:** Manual editing & repair workspace, algorithmic theory-based generation, cross-platform accessibility.
